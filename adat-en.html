ADAT

A lifestyle brand concept bringing together contemporary aesthetics and the heritage of the Caucasus. It centres on expeditions through the highlands, gathering herbs for tea and sharing tea rituals along the way.

The brief and research

The aim was to bring travel, tea and Caucasian culture together in one brand. The concept centres on the experience of an explorer discovering a region through its routes, flavours and local traditions.

Research compared adventure clubs, boutique tours and tea brands. This informed a positioning in which an expedition and a tea ritual become parts of the same journey.

Visual language

The exploration considered line drawings, landscapes and animal imagery. Botanical illustrations became the foundation of the final identity, connecting the graphics to herb gathering and tea. Mountain landscapes establish the setting for the journey.

Muted green, blue and pink distinguish the different design variations. Enlarged botanical details appear on packaging and posters, while smaller illustrations carry over to stickers and badges.

The tea ritual

The graphics extend to tableware, packaging and printed materials. The tea ritual shares the expedition's visual language, and individual objects come together as one coherent brand.

Bringing the journey home

Notebooks, postcards, stickers and badges carry the identity onto objects to take on a trip or keep afterwards. Botanical motifs recur at different scales and across different formats.

Website

The website introduces the brand and helps visitors choose a trip. They can explore expeditions, read about past participants' experiences and book a journey. Landscapes reveal the routes, while botanical graphics connect the website to the packaging and printed materials.

The result

The project develops an identity for expeditions and tea rituals, shown across packaging, tableware, souvenirs, promotional materials and a website. A shared graphic language connects discovering a route, taking the trip and remembering it afterwards.
